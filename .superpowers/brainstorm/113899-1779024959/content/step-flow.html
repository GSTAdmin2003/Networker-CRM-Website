How should the two-step form flow?

Phase 1 captures the phone number. Phase 2 is optional company details. Pick the transition style that feels right.

Progress bar at top. "Next" slides to step 2. A "skip / just submit" link below sends with only the phone. Step 2 has a "Back" link and a "Submit" button.

Phone at top with Send button. Below a divider: optional fields visible but clearly secondary. No steps — user decides how much to fill in. Simpler but less guided.
