The core problem

The Problem section is a 2-column grid on desktop but collapses to 1-column on mobile — its content stacks vertically, growing its height by ~40%. Combined with the Waitlist section, navy exceeds 30% at smaller viewports. The fix: control how much vertical space dark sections can occupy at each breakpoint using max-height, min-height: Xsvh, or tighter padding at mobile.
